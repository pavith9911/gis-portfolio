Method

Each criterion is rescaled to a 0 to 1 suitability value with a simple reclass function, then combined as a weighted sum and expressed on a 0 to 100 scale. Cells that break a hard constraint are excluded.

  • Irradiation: 4.8 kWh/m²/day scores 0, 5.4 or more scores 1
  • Slope: up to 2° scores 1, falling to 0 at 10°
  • Grid distance: within 3 km scores 1, falling to 0 at 30 km
  • Road distance: within 1 km scores 1, falling to 0 at 10 km

For a real study, replace the synthetic grids with SRTM slope, a solar-resource raster (e.g. Global Solar Atlas), distance rasters from the national grid and road network, and land-cover or protected-area masks, all resampled to one grid in QGIS.
