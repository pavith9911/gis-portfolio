Method

Coordinates are parsed from decimal, decimal-comma and degrees-minutes-seconds notation. Points outside the Sri Lanka bounding box are retried with latitude and longitude swapped; if they still fall outside they are rejected. Districts come from point-in-polygon tests against simplified geoBoundaries ADM2 polygons (CC BY 4.0); points that fall in a coastal sliver outside the simplified outline are kept and flagged (unmatched). Categories are matched to a controlled list with fuzzy matching. Duplicates are records with the same normalised name and category within 50 m of an earlier record.
